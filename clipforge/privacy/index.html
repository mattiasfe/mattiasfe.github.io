ClipForge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Summary

ClipForge is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It collects only what it needs to post clips to the owner's connected accounts. It does not sell, share or rent data, and it does not show ads.

2. Data from TikTok

When the owner connects a TikTok account, ClipForge receives:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, to post approved clips to that account.
  • A list of the account's own videos and their view counts, to confirm that a post went live and to track its performance.

ClipForge does not access other users' data, direct messages, followers or contacts.

3. Other data

ClipForge also stores the clips it creates, their captions and posting history, and similar data for any connected YouTube account. Source videos come from public Twitch streams.

4. Where data is stored

All data is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Rendered clips are uploaded to TikTok or YouTube only when the owner has approved them for posting.

5. Third parties

Data is sent only to the platforms the owner posts to (TikTok, YouTube) and, for clip analysis, to an AI service that receives clip transcripts and still frames. No data is shared for advertising or marketing.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. The owner can disconnect a TikTok account at any time by revoking ClipForge's access in TikTok's settings; ClipForge then deletes the stored tokens on request. Clips and posting history can be deleted by the owner at any time.

7. Children

ClipForge is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent as an issue on GitHub.
