ClipForge

Short clips from long streams

A personal tool for turning Twitch streams into vertical short-form videos.

What it does

  • Finds the most interesting moments in a finished Twitch stream.
  • Cuts them into short vertical clips with captions.
  • Lets the owner review each clip before anything is posted.
  • Posts approved clips to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Who it is for

ClipForge is a private tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and posts only to accounts the owner has connected. It is not a public service, and there is no sign-up.

TikTok

ClipForge uses TikTok Login Kit so the owner can connect their own TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to post clips they have approved to that account. It reads the account's own videos only to confirm that a post went live and to track its views.
